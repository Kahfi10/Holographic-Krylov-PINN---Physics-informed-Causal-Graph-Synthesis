# Academic Publishing Standards & Manuscript Quality Criteria

This reference outlines the technical, stylistic, and formatting standards required for successful submission to leading scientific journals (e.g., IEEE Transactions, Elsevier, Springer, IJEA, JANAPATI).

---

## 1. Manuscript Architecture & Section Progression

1. **Title**: Concise, informative, specifying methodology, problem domain, and application context.
2. **Abstract**:
   - Structured in 4 core components: Background/Problem, Proposed Architecture/Methodology, Empirical Telemetry/Setup, and Exact Quantitative Milestones.
   - Must contain concrete numerical values (e.g., Reward 41.91, $\sigma = 0.0130$, Dev Sat 52.88%, +23.9% gain, convergence speed).
3. **Introduction**:
   - Real-world motivation and industrial pain points.
   - Identified research gap synthesized in a **Related Works Table** (Table 1).
   - Explicit numbered list of contributions (First, ... Second, ... Third, ... Fourth, ...).
4. **Research Methodology**:
   - Architectural framework diagram (Figure 1).
   - Empirical telemetry mining and feature specification schema (Table 2).
   - Mathematical modeling (state space, action space, reward functions, behavioral penalties).
   - Parameter calibration table with physical/behavioral interpretations (Table 3).
   - Neural network architecture and hyperparameter specifications (Table 4).
   - Formal evaluation metrics definitions (Table 5).
5. **Results and Discussion**:
   - Convergence analysis (Figure 2).
   - Comparative empirical performance benchmarks across 30 test sprints (Table 6).
   - Behavioral stress testing and backlog volatility correlation (Figure 3).
   - Temporal stability heatmap (Figure 4).
   - Multi-criteria trade-off and workload concentration Pareto curves (Figure 5).
   - Theoretical and practical enterprise governance implications.
6. **Conclusions and Recommendations**:
   - Synthesis of verified quantitative outcomes.
   - Practical takeaways for engineering managers / Scrum Masters.
   - Specific future research directions (MARL, NLP sentiment, live Jira integrations).
7. **References**: Full APA 7th Edition with complete DOIs.

---

## 2. Mandatory Rules

- **Zero Bullet Points in Body**: All narrative must be continuous, coherent academic prose.
- **Dual-Mention Rule**: Every table and figure must be referenced both before (pre-mention) and after (post-mention analytical discussion).
- **600 DPI Figure Standards**: Times New Roman / Cambria font, minimum 12pt label size, zero visual clipping.
- **Office Math Standard**: All equations and inline variables rendered as native Word `<m:oMath>` objects.
